# A promoted law recurs as casualties until enforced at its choke point

## Observation (paid for)

C1 — mint credentials scoped to the FULL job — is already promoted canon
(klappy://ars/policy/seat-minted-flight-credentials). Yet on 2026-08-04 the
container lane minted a git-push-only `ghs_` token for a job whose declared
deliverable was a PR. The flight pushed its branch and could not open the
PR. This is the THIRD casualty of the same gap (carried item 6 across
sweeps): the law exists, the mint doesn't consult it.

## Candidate principle

Promotion into canon changes what agents can cite, not what machinery does.
A law whose violations keep occurring at one mechanical point (here: the
dispatch-time token mint) is not enforced until that point checks it — the
mint must refuse or widen scope when the brief's declared deliverable
(open a PR) exceeds the token's grant (push only). Until the choke point
enforces, each recurrence costs a flight and a sweep to detect; the fix
belongs in the door, not in another reminder.

## Why (trust kernel)

Integrity is non-negotiable efficiency (Axiom 3): three lost flights and
three triage passes cost more than one guard at the mint. Auditable
expectations require the enforcement to live where the expectation is set.
