# Candidate: retrieval aliases land with canon — vocabulary is part of the promotion

**Claim.** A canon doc is not fully landed until the names people will
actually use for it retrieve it. Promotions should ship likely aliases
(paraphrases, ordinal forms, colloquial names) as frontmatter tags at landing
time. The promotion pipeline gains a checklist line: "what will a human call
this six weeks from now? Tag it."

**Evidence.**
1. 2026-07-20 — "third loop" (a natural paraphrase of "triple-loop") ranked
   the just-ratified triple-loop-debrief-review doc **4th**, behind three
   generic loop docs, against a *fresh* index; in a stale window it surfaced
   nothing at all. A second session missed the doc entirely and improvised.
2. Fix shipped same day: alias tags `triple-loop`, `third-loop`,
   `three-loops` (klappy.dev PR #309) — this candidate generalizes that fix
   into pipeline law.

**Relation.** Sibling of the held dual-context-vocabulary seed (sweep
2026-07-20): that one is about one term meaning two things; this one is about
one thing carrying two names. Both are naming-discipline; a future promotion
may fold them.

**Status.** Single incident, honestly flagged; the alias PR is the worked
example.
